📝 Data Format & Structure

CAMS Standard Format

Society,Year,Node,Coherence,Capacity,Stress,Abstraction,Node Value,Bond Strength
USA,2025,Executive,5,6,3,7,12.5,2.1
USA,2025,Military,5,7,2,6,13.0,2.2
USA,2025,Knowledge,7,8,1,8,18.5,2.8
...

Node Classifications

  • Executive: Central decision-making authority
  • Military: Defense and security apparatus
  • Knowledge: Education and research institutions
  • Property: Ownership and investment classes
  • Trades: Skilled professions and crafts
  • Labor: Manual work and primary production
  • Archives: Information storage and memory
  • Merchants: Trade and distribution networks

Variable Ranges

  • Coherence (C): 0-10 (institutional coordination)
  • Capacity (K): 0-10 (operational capability)
  • Stress (S): 0 to 10 (system pressure)
  • Abstraction (A): 0-10 (complexity handling)
