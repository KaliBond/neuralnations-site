🌐 Power Type Attractor Space

This interactive 3D visualization shows how different power types create distinct attractor patterns in CAMS phase space. Each trajectory represents a different civilizational power structure evolving through the dimensions of Abstraction, Stress, and Control Capacity.

🔬 Technocratic
Knowledge-driven governance, moderate complexity
⛪ Theocratic
Religious authority, high institutional stability
⚔️ Militarist
Military-dominated, high stress tolerance
💰 Plutocratic
Wealth-concentrated power, economic focus
🗳️ Democratic
Distributed power, adaptive institutions
🏴‍☠️ Kleptocratic
Extractive governance, high instability

🔍 How to Interpret the Visualization

  • Trajectory Patterns: Each colored line shows how a power type evolves through the three-dimensional state space
  • Attractor Basins: Stable regions where trajectories converge represent sustainable governance patterns
  • Chaotic Regions: Areas where trajectories diverge indicate institutional instability
  • Dimensional Meaning: X-axis (Abstraction), Y-axis (Stress), Z-axis (Control Capacity)

Rotate, zoom, and explore the 3D space to discover how different power structures create distinct patterns in civilizational dynamics.
