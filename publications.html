📚 Core Framework Papers

CAMS Framework v2.1: Stress-Modulated Neural Networks for Societal Analysis

In Preparation
Kari McKern

This paper presents the production-ready implementation of the Complex Adaptive Model of Societies (CAMS) framework v2.1, featuring stress-modulated neural network dynamics for analyzing institutional structures across civilizations. The framework models societies as 8-node neural networks with dynamic plasticity governed by stress, coherence, and capacity variables.

Key Contributions: Mathematical formalization of stress-gated plasticity, validation against historical datasets, and demonstration of predictive capabilities for civilizational transitions.

Complex Adaptive Systems Neural Networks Institutional Analysis Civilizational Dynamics

Societies as Stress-Modulated Neural Networks: A Mathematical Framework

Under Review
Kari McKern

Theoretical foundation for modeling societal institutions as interconnected neural networks where learning and adaptation are modulated by system-wide stress. Introduces the Grand System Metric (Ψ) and mathematical specifications for institutional node interactions.
